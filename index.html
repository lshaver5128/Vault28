⚡ Seller Guide & FAQ

Where to Sell Pokémon Cards

Looking for the best place to sell your Pokémon card collection? Here is how Vault 28 Trading Co. gives collectors transparent market valuation, instant cash payouts, and a hassle-free experience.

📍

Where can I sell my Pokémon card collection?

Vault 28 Trading Co. is the premier destination to sell Pokémon cards directly for immediate cash without paying 13%–15% marketplace seller fees. We buy vintage WOTC (Base Set, Jungle, Fossil, Team Rocket), modern secret rares, raw holos, and entire binders with zero hassle.

🤝

Can I sell locally near Summerville & Charleston, SC?

Yes! We regularly arrange safe, in-person cash transactions at local public locations (police station safe exchange zones, banks, or local hobby centers) throughout Summerville, Charleston, Mount Pleasant, and surrounding Lowcountry areas. Just text or email us with photos to set up a private evaluation.

📦

Can I sell Pokémon cards by mail nationwide?

Yes, we buy collections nationwide across the United States. Simply text photos to 774-424-3561, email us, or DM @Vault28Cards on Instagram. We agree on an offer upfront, you ship with tracking, and upon arrival and verification, payment is issued immediately via Cash, Zelle, PayPal, or Venmo.

💰

How much does Vault 28 pay for Pokémon cards?

We offer industry-leading payouts: 65% to 75% in cash for raw vintage & modern singles ($15+ value), and 70% to 80% in trade credit towards cards in our inventory. For complete collections and binders, we provide custom bulk cash offers so you don't have to spend months trying to piece out your collection.

⚖️

Why sell to Vault 28 instead of eBay or pawn shops?

Selling on eBay takes weeks of listing work, packing individual envelopes, and paying 13%–15% in platform fees, plus the risk of shipping damage, chargebacks, or return scams. Pawn shops typically only offer 30%–40% because they lack specialized Pokémon knowledge. At Vault 28, you get true collector expertise, transparent market pricing, and same-day payment with zero seller fees.

🔍

What pricing guides does Vault 28 use?

Every evaluation is 100% transparent. We cross-reference real-time market data from TCGplayer Market Price and eBay Recently Sold comps for the exact condition of your raw cards (Near Mint, Lightly Played, Moderately Played). You always see the numbers behind our offer.

⚡ The Founder's Journey

The Story Behind Vault 28

From marketplace hustling to uncovering vintage 1st Edition treasures, to going all-in on Pokémon.

How It All Started

“Vault 28 started from a genuine passion for the hunt. What began as browsing local sports card collections on Facebook Marketplace turned into an unexpected discovery of vintage 1st Edition Pokémon holos — and that moment changed everything.”

📦 1. Starting Off with Sports Cards on Marketplace

I originally began by hunting sports card collections and lots across Facebook Marketplace. I loved digging through binders, learning card conditions, analyzing comps, and offering straightforward, fair cash buyouts to folks looking to clean off their shelves.

✨ 2. The Breakthrough: Vintage 1st Edition Holos

Then came the collection that changed everything. I took a chance on an unassuming deal and got incredibly lucky: it was packed to the brim with vintage 1st Edition Pokémon cards and iconic WOTC holos. Holding those childhood grails reignited an obsession. The nostalgic energy, community passion, and timeless artwork in Pokémon completely blew sports cards out of the water.

🎪 3. Going All-In & Hitting the Road Vending

From that turning point, I made the decision to pivot 100% into Pokémon. I launched Vault 28 Trading Co., invested in a full live vending booth setup, and hit the road at card conventions and shows. Today, we're proud to buy raw collections on the spot for cash, trade grails from our inventory, and connect directly with fellow collectors.

🛠️ Connect Google Firebase

Step 1: Go to Firebase Console Open console.firebase.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
Step 2: Create a Project Click Add Project, name it (e.g. Vault28), disable Google Analytics (recommended for fast setup), and click Create.
Step 3: Register a Web App In your project overview, click the Web icon (</>) to register an app. Name it Vault28Web and click register.
Step 4: Copy Config Keys Firebase will show you a configuration script. Copy only the firebaseConfig object. It looks like this: 
const firebaseConfig = {
  apiKey: "AIzaSy...",
  authDomain: "vault28.firebaseapp.com",
  projectId: "vault28",
  storageBucket: "vault28.appspot.com",
  messagingSenderId: "123456...",
  appId: "1:123456..."
};
Step 5: Paste in app.js Open app.js in VS Code and paste your config into the FIREBASE_CONFIG variable at the very top of the file. Save and push the change to your GitHub repo!
